# Beck Atlantic: Higgsfield image prompts

Routing: higgsfield-prompt + higgsfield-image-shots, still images. Models and aspect ratios checked against the skill's image spec snapshot dated 2026-09-26.

These are for the **atmosphere images** on the site: the hero banner and the three photo slots. **Use real photos for the team section.** An AI-generated face on a "meet the founders" section breaks the very trust that section is meant to build.

All four use the same look so they sit together as one set:
*Style: Cinematic. Deep navy shadows, warm brass highlights, restrained contrast.*

---

### 01 Hero: trade-show floor at opening hour
**Model**: Nano Banana 2
**Aspect ratio**: 21:9  **Resolution**: 2k

Wide shot, eye-level, static camera. A modern exhibition hall minutes before doors open: rows of clean white booths with blank fascia panels, a single booth in the foreground holding a large medical imaging gantry under a fabric cover. Two exhibitors in dark suits adjust the cover, backs half-turned to camera. Polished concrete floor reflecting overhead light trusses, long aisles receding toward glass entrance doors.
Lighting: cool overhead hall light, warm spill from booth downlights on the foreground.
Style: Cinematic. Deep navy shadows, warm brass highlights, restrained contrast. Sharp focus throughout, deep depth of field. Signage panels blank, no readable text.

### 03 Image slot 2: the meeting at the booth
**Model**: Nano Banana 2
**Aspect ratio**: 4:5  **Resolution**: 2k

Medium shot, over-the-shoulder. An engineer in a navy blazer points at a control console beside a medical imaging system while a visitor wearing a lanyard leans in to read the screen, hand on chin. The booth wall behind is matte white with a soft brass edge light. Natural skin texture, subtle imperfections.
Lighting: soft key from booth downlights, single warm side-light.
Style: Cinematic. Deep navy shadows, warm brass highlights, restrained contrast. Subject in sharp focus, background falling into soft bokeh. Console screen shows abstract grey scan imagery, no readable text.

### 04 Image slot 3: the Atlantic from above
**Model**: Nano Banana 2

Overhead extreme wide shot of open Atlantic water from cruising altitude, a long swell pattern in deep blue-black, a thin line of cloud shadow crossing the frame diagonally, one cargo vessel's wake as a tiny pale line near the lower third.

Style: Cinematic. Deep navy shadows, warm brass highlights, restrained contrast. Sharp focus throughout.
