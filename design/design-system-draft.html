Black and brass

The system behind the draft. Blacks never meet in a hard edge; they blend. Brushed brass is the only colour, used sparingly so it shines when it appears.

Black field

Seven blacks from #050505 to #2C2C2C. Neighbouring surfaces are at most two steps apart and always meet in a gradient.

Sampled from your brushed brass. Brushed horizontally for buttons and bars, polished vertically for type, numbers and the logo. A fine line texture gives it the brushed grain.

Surfaces and light

Panels are solid black, a soft gradient from #171717 to #121212, set off from the field by a tone step and a faint lit top edge. Light comes from soft spotlights; on cards a faint light follows the pointer.

Rules

  • No colour besides black and brass, including the logo.
  • Two blacks never meet in a hard line; use a gradient or a soft tone step between them.
  • Polished brass only for the main action, key words, figures and the needle.
  • Running text is flat warm white #EEE9DF or #B5AC9C, never metallic, so it stays readable.
  • Every tap target is at least 44px; no text below 14px.
  • No invented proof: logos, quotes and prices in [brackets] wait for real ones.
