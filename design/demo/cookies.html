What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that a website places on your computer or phone when you visit it. They can help a site work, remember your preferences, or show how the site is used. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them. First-party cookies are set by the site you visit, and third-party cookies are set by other companies, such as analytics or advertising providers.

Other technologies

We also do not use similar technologies: no browser storage (such as localStorage), no web beacons or tracking pixels, no session-recording tools and no third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from our own website, so your browser is not sent to another company to load them.

Like every website, the Site’s hosting provider receives basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page. That is not a cookie. Our privacy policy explains how this information is handled.

Your choices

Because the Site sets no cookies, you do not need to change anything. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies in their settings, which you can do for any website. Your browser’s help pages explain how.

Changes

If we ever add cookies or similar technologies, we will update this notice first and ask for your consent before setting anything that is not strictly necessary.
