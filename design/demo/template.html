Test America in one room.

A trade show is a controlled place to meet the US market before you commit to it. In a few days you learn more than in a year of emails.

  • Get leadsDistributors, buyers and partners, met face to face.
  • Research your competitorsSee who sells what, at what price, and how they pitch it.
